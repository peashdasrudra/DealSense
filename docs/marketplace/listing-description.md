## App Name
DealSense Revenue Intelligence

## Tagline
Autonomous deal risk intelligence, velocity scoring, and automated RevOps playbooks.

## Description (Public)
Most B2B companies lose revenue every quarter to silent pipeline slippage. Reps forecast based on gut feeling, key stakeholders disengage, and decision-making stalls. 

DealSense replaces subjective guesswork with a **deterministic mathematical scoring engine**. By analyzing deep CRM telemetry (stage aging, engagement decay, stakeholder coverage, and CRM hygiene), DealSense calculates a purely objective 0–100 Deal Health Score. 

**Key Features:**
* **Native HubSpot UI**: View intelligence directly inside your HubSpot Deal records (Tab and Sidebar cards).
* **Zero-Hallucination Scoring**: Our proprietary 7-vector algorithm ensures 100% reproducible and explainable scores.
* **MEDDICC Qualification Matrix**: Automatically tracks identified and missing stakeholders or criteria based on recent activity.
* **Proactive Risk Signals**: Instantly highlights critical risks such as "18 Days of Stakeholder Silence" or "Missing Economic Buyer."
* **Command Deck**: A dedicated dashboard for Revenue Leaders to approve AI-recommended next-best-actions (like drafting an executive summary or adjusting forecast dates).

Stop relying on generic AI summaries and start commanding your pipeline with objective mathematics.
