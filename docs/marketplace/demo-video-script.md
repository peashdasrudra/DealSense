# DealSense — HubSpot App Marketplace Demo Video Script

**Target Duration**: 2 minutes  
**Audience**: HubSpot Marketplace Review Team, RevOps Leaders, Sales Directors  

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] Introduction & The Problem
- **Visual**: Open HubSpot Deals Board with dozens of deals in various stages.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Every quarter, B2B sales teams lose high-value deals to silent pipeline decay. Reps forecast based on gut feel, economic buyers disengage without notice, and close dates slip. Today, we're introducing DealSense — the first HubSpot-native AI deal intelligence platform built on deterministic mathematical scoring."*

---

### [0:25 - 0:55] One-Click Install & Native UI Extension
- **Visual**: Quick demo of OAuth authorization, then navigating to a deal in HubSpot.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Installing DealSense takes seconds through standard HubSpot OAuth. Once connected, DealSense seamlessly injects native UI Extension cards directly into your HubSpot Deal records without any messy iframes or context switching."*
- **Visual**: Zoom in on the Deal Sidebar Card showing the 0–100 Health Score and top risk factor.

---

### [0:55 - 1:35] Deep Deal Intelligence Tab & MEDDICC Matrix
- **Visual**: Click on the "DealSense Intelligence" tab in the Deal record.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Opening the DealSense Intelligence tab gives RevOps leaders complete clarity. Our 7-signal deterministic algorithm calculates a health score with zero hallucinations. We see the risk narrative, the score trend delta, and expandable risk signals detailing stage aging or stakeholder silence."*
- **Visual**: Scroll through the MEDDICC Qualification Matrix table showing Champion, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria status tags.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"DealSense automatically populates the MEDDICC qualification matrix from your CRM activities and notes, surfacing exactly which stakeholders are missing before the quarter ends."*

---

### [1:35 - 1:55] Autonomous RevOps & Command Deck Write-Backs
- **Visual**: Click "Open Command Deck for Approvals" button to open the DealSense Dashboard.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Revenue leaders can review next-best-action proposals in the DealSense Command Deck, approve playbook tasks, and execute controlled write-backs directly into HubSpot with full audit logging."*

---

### [1:55 - 2:00] Conclusion & Call to Action
- **Visual**: DealSense logo, Marketplace badge, and `https://dealsense.peash.tech`.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Command your revenue pipeline with objective intelligence. Install DealSense from the HubSpot App Marketplace today."*
