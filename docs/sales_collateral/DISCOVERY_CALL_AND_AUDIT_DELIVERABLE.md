## ⏱️ Part 1: The 15-Minute Discovery Call Protocol

### Minute 0–2: Context Setting & Framing
> *"Thanks for jumping on, [First Name]. I specialize in HubSpot-native revenue engineering and white-label AI/RevOps systems for agencies. Rather than giving you a canned pitch, my goal today is simply to understand how your team handles technical CRM requests, where you see delivery friction, and see if a small 5-day workflow sprint or pilot audit could take work off your plate. Does that sound fair?"*

---

### Minute 2–7: Money-Finding Discovery Questions
Ask 3–4 targeted questions to uncover high-friction, billable client bottlenecks:
1. *"What types of HubSpot implementation or RevOps requests do your clients ask for most frequently?"*
2. *"When a client asks for custom API integrations, webhook logic, or AI workflows beyond native HubSpot, how do you handle it today?"*
3. *"Where do your clients struggle most with CRM data hygiene or deals stalling invisibly in their pipelines?"*
4. *"Have you had client projects delayed or turned down due to internal technical capacity bottlenecks?"*
5. *"If we could eliminate one specific delivery bottleneck in the next 5 business days, what would be most valuable to your agency?"*

---

### Minute 7–11: Laser-Focused Live Proof (Screen Share)
Share **only the specific module on `dealsense.peash.tech`** that matches their stated problem:

| Client Stated Pain Point | Module to Screen Share | Key Feature to Highlight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Deals stall silently / reps guess forecasts"** | `/pipeline` or `/forecast` | 7-vector deterministic risk badge & Monte Carlo probability distribution. |
| **"CFOs ghosting / single-threaded deals"** | `/stakeholders` or `/war-room` | Multi-threading power matrix & Economic Buyer 14-day silence warning. |
| **"Messy CRM data / close dates constantly slipping"** | `/hygiene` | 1-click batch date slip auto-remediation & discrepancy detector. |

| **"Need to offer new high-ticket retainers to clients"** | `/case-study` | White-label client fleet model ($2.5K/mo retainer arbitrage). |

---

### Minute 11–14: Transition to Low-Risk Micro-Offer
> *"Based on what you mentioned around [specific problem, e.g., deals stalling without active next steps], the cleanest low-risk way to test our technical workflow is a $99 Pilot Deal Risk Audit (or a 5-day Revenue Workflow Sprint).*
> 
> *I audit one active pipeline (up to 50 deals), calculate deterministic 7-vector risk scores, identify the top 10 at-risk opportunities, and deliver a 1-page C-level report with a 10-minute Loom walkthrough in 3–5 business days.*
> 
> *It's backed by a 100% money-back guarantee if we don't flag at least $25K in at-risk pipeline. Would it make sense for us to test this on one pipeline?"*

---

### Minute 14–15: Clear Close & Action Item
Secure one of these concrete outcomes:
* ✅ *"Great! I’ll send over the 1-page scope and read-only OAuth link right after this call."*
* ✅ *"Let's schedule the Loom review for next Tuesday at 10 AM."*
* ❌ If not a fit: *"Totally understand. I'll send our technical capabilities overview to keep on file for future overflow needs."*
