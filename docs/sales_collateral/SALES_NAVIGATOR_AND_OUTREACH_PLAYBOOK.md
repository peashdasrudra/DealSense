### B. 5 Saved Lead Searches & Boolean Query Strings

#### Search 1: Small HubSpot Agency Founders / Owners
* **Boolean String:**
  ```text
  (HubSpot OR RevOps OR "Revenue Operations" OR "HubSpot Partner") AND (founder OR owner OR CEO OR partner OR principal)
  ```
* **Filters:**
  * **Geography:** United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia
  * **Industry:** Marketing Services, Advertising Services, Management Consulting, IT Services & IT Consulting, Business Consulting
  * **Company Headcount:** 5–50 employees
  * **Seniority Level:** Owner, Partner, CXO
  * **Function:** Entrepreneurship, Operations, Consulting, Business Development
  * **Alert Frequency:** Weekly email alerts

#### Search 2: RevOps Delivery Leaders & Directors
* **Boolean String:**
  ```text
  ("Head of RevOps" OR "Director of RevOps" OR "Revenue Operations" OR COO OR "Client Services Director" OR "Operations Director") AND (HubSpot OR CRM OR "sales operations")
  ```
* **Filters:**
  * **Geography:** US, UK, Canada, Australia
  * **Company Headcount:** 10–100 employees
  * **Industry:** Marketing Services, Consulting, Software Development, IT Services
  * **Years in Current Role:** 0–5 years
  * **Spotlight:** Posted on LinkedIn in past 30 days

#### Search 3: Technical Decision-Makers & Architects
* **Boolean String:**
  ```text
  ("Technical Director" OR CTO OR "Solutions Architect" OR "Integration Lead" OR "Technical Consultant" OR "HubSpot Developer") AND (HubSpot OR "CRM integration" OR API OR webhook)
  ```
* **Filters:**
  * **Geography:** US, UK, Canada, Australia
  * **Company Headcount:** 10–100 employees

#### Search 4: Independent & Fractional RevOps Consultants
* **Boolean String:**
  ```text
  ("HubSpot Consultant" OR "Fractional RevOps" OR "Revenue Operations Consultant" OR "HubSpot Specialist") NOT ("seeking opportunities" OR student)
  ```
* **Filters:**
  * **Company Headcount:** 1–10 employees (Self-employed / Boutique)
  * **Geography:** US, UK, Canada, Australia

#### Search 5: Direct B2B Revenue Leaders
* **Boolean String:**
  ```text
  ("VP Sales" OR "Head of Sales" OR CRO OR "Head of RevOps" OR "Director of Sales Operations") AND (HubSpot OR "HubSpot CRM")
  ```
* **Filters:**
  * **Company Headcount:** 20–500 employees
  * **Industry:** SaaS, Enterprise Software, Cybersecurity, IT Services
  * **Growth Trigger:** Hiring in Sales / RevOps

## 🔄 3. Multi-Touch Follow-Up Sequence (After Acceptance)

### Touch 1 (Day 0 — Within 2–4 Hours of Acceptance)
```text
Thanks for connecting, [First Name].

I'm focused on helping HubSpot/RevOps agencies with the technical work that becomes difficult once native workflows are not enough — custom API integrations, data-quality automation, secure webhooks, and AI-assisted CRM workflows.

I am currently building DealSense, a HubSpot-native deal-risk system that identifies stalled deals and proposes approved next actions. I'm speaking with a small number of agency leaders to understand which delivery bottlenecks are most valuable to solve.

Is custom HubSpot engineering something your agency currently handles internally, partners out, or avoids unless necessary?
```

### Touch 2 (Day 2–3 — Relevant Value & Problem Insight)
*Send if no reply to Day 0:*
```text
One common issue I keep seeing in HubSpot sales pipelines is that "no next step" and close-date slippage are visible only after a manager manually reviews deals.

I'm building a small workflow that flags those cases automatically and creates an approval-ready task.

If your team ever needs overflow support for something similar, I can share a short 90-second demo.
```

### Touch 3 (Day 5–7 — The $99–$250 Micro-Offer / 5-Day Sprint)
```text
I have a small fixed-scope offer for HubSpot/RevOps agencies: a 5-day Revenue Workflow Sprint.

I audit one client pipeline, identify the highest-impact process or data-quality gap, and implement one working automation or integration fix with handover documentation.

For early agency partners, I'm offering the first sprint at $99–$250 so there is very little risk. Would it be useful if I sent the one-page scope?
```

### Touch 4 (Day 10–12 — Permission-Based Follow-up)
```text
Hi [First Name], should I send the one-page workflow sprint scope, or is custom HubSpot/AI workflow work not a priority for your team at the moment?
```

### Touch 5 (Day 16–20 — Graceful Close of Loop)
```text
I'll close the loop here, [First Name].

If a client ever needs a custom HubSpot integration, workflow reliability fix, native UI extension, or AI-assisted CRM workflow, I would be glad to be a white-label technical resource.

I'll keep sharing practical HubSpot engineering notes here as I build. Thanks again for connecting!
```

## 🛡️ 5. Objection Handling Battle-Scripts

| Prospect Objection | Exact Winning Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"We already do HubSpot workflows ourselves."** | *"That makes total sense. I don't replace standard workflows. I handle the edge cases that require custom APIs, secure webhooks, deterministic math scoring, or native UI extensions where no-code breaks down. Happy to be an on-demand white-label overflow resource when complex requests hit your plate."* |
| **"We do not have an active project right now."** | *"Understood! Rather than push for a project, would it be helpful if I shared our 1-page technical capabilities sheet? You can keep it on file whenever a client asks for custom CRM intelligence or complex API integrations."* |
| **"We already have in-house developers."** | *"That's great. I work alongside internal teams during capacity crunches or for specialized HubSpot API/FastAPI modules. Clear PRs, 100% test coverage, zero management overhead."* |
| **"AI is not currently our priority."** | *"Completely understood. Most of what I deliver isn't generative AI — it's deterministic CRM hygiene: catching stalled deals, flagging ghosting CFOs, and automating close-date slip corrections. Pure RevOps efficiency."* |
| **"Why is your pilot price only $99?"** | *"Because we're validating our agency partner onboarding flow and gathering anonymized case study data. The scope is strictly bounded to 50 deals in 48 hours. Full production deployments are $1,500–$3,500."* |
| **"Can you do it for free as a trial?"** | *"I can give you a live 15-minute screen share demo for free anytime. For an actual client pipeline audit, the $99 fee ensures mutual prioritization and covers an executive PDF deliverable and 10-minute video walkthrough."* |
